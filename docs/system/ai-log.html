What the AI did: Read the handout, compared it with the native app, and found that the native app could not meet the browser, public link, and no-paid-service requirements. It then wrote the requirements, business rules, screens, diagrams, and tests.

Decisions the student owns: The target user, the value model, moving from a native app to a browser prototype, replacing the Claude API with rule-based cleanup, and keeping paste and proofreading manual. The business rule limits for word count, similarity, and retention were adapted from the native app or chosen for this prototype, and the student must be able to explain each one.
